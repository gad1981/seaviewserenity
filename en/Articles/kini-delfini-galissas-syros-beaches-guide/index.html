Kini, Delfini, and Galissas: A Practical Syros Beaches Guide

By Sea View Serenity hosts · Updated 2026-11-07 · ~6–8 min read

Beach keywords drive a large share of Syros accommodation searches: Kini beach, Delfini beach Syros, Galissas beach, and “best beaches Syros.” Official guidance is the right starting point before blog rankings.

Galissas: the easy all-rounder

Visit Greece describes Galissas as a large bay with fine sand and easy access, with the chapel of Agia Pakou at the far end—one of the island’s most practical full beach-day settings, including accessibility features listed for the site.[1]

Kini: sunset and taverna energy

Kini combines a shallow sandy cove with waterfront tavernas and a strong sunset reputation. It is also listed with accessibility facilities, which matters for mixed-age groups.[1]

Delfini: quieter and closer to a “stay local” rhythm

Delfini and Varvarousa are presented as smaller west-coast coves near Kini, reached by a short walk along the road or by boat.[1] That is exactly why travellers hunting quieter seaside accommodation often prefer a house in this pocket: less transfer time, more repeat swimming, fewer logistics.
