Welcome

Welcome to Sea View Serenity, your exclusive coastal escape just a leisurely 10-min walk from Delfini beach at Syros island. Ideal for families or groups seeking a serene getaway, our charming house offers cozy accommodations and ample space to unwind. Enjoy candlelit dinners with breathtaking sea views from your bedrooms. With umbrellas and a beach bar nearby, endless days of sun-soaked bliss await. Whether lounging on the shore, hiking trails, or savoring local seafood, our home is your perfect coastal base.

The View

Our seaside house offers breathtaking views that perfectly capture the essence of coastal beauty. From the spacious windows, you can gaze out over the expansive aegean sea, watching the waves gently lap against the shore. The picturesque scenery is complemented by stunning sunsets that paint the sky in vibrant hues, providing a serene backdrop for relaxation. Whether you're enjoying a morning coffee or unwinding in the evening, the view from our house promises to be a constant source of tranquility and inspiration.

Amenities

Our seaside house offers a range of top-notch amenities to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay. Guests can take advantage of the two dedicated parking slots under a protective ceiling, making arrivals and departures seamless. The property includes complimentary Wi-Fi coverage throughout, allowing you to stay connected. The fully equipped kitchen is perfect for preparing meals, featuring a large fridge with freezer, electric stove, kettle, toaster, microwave, and French coffee maker. Inside, the second bathroom features a sculptured marble-framed mirror, adding a touch of elegance, and includes a washing machine for added convenience. The house also boasts cozy bedrooms with plush bedding, an indoor fire pit for evening gatherings, and easy access to a nearby beach bar and amenities.
