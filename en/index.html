Welcome

Welcome to Sea View Serenity, your private coastal escape on Syros, just a leisurely 10-minute walk from Delfini Beach. Ideal for families or groups looking for a peaceful getaway, our charming house offers cozy rooms and plenty of space to unwind. Enjoy candlelit dinners and sea views from the bedrooms. With umbrellas and a beach bar nearby, long sunny days are never far away. Whether you're lounging on the shore, hiking the trails or tucking into local seafood, this is the perfect coastal base.

The View

Our seaside house has views that capture the best of the coast. From the spacious windows you look out over the Aegean, with waves lapping gently at the shore. Sunsets paint the sky in rich colours, a calm backdrop for unwinding. Whether it's a morning coffee or a quiet evening, the view is a constant source of calm and inspiration.

Amenities

Everything is set up for an easy stay. There are two parking spaces under a covered roof, free Wi‑Fi throughout, and a fully equipped kitchen with a large fridge-freezer, electric stove, kettle, toaster, microwave and French coffee maker. The second bathroom has a sculpted marble-framed mirror and a washing machine. You'll also find cozy bedrooms with plush bedding, an indoor fire pit for evening gatherings, and easy access to a nearby beach bar.
