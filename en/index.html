Welcome

Welcome to Sea View Serenity, your exclusive coastal escape just a leisurely 10-minute walk from Delfini Beach on Syros. Ideal for families or groups seeking a serene getaway, our charming house offers cozy accommodations and ample space to unwind. Enjoy candlelit dinners with breathtaking sea views from your bedrooms. With umbrellas and a beach bar nearby, endless days of sun-soaked bliss await. Whether lounging on the shore, hiking trails, or savouring local seafood, our home is your perfect coastal base.

The View

Our seaside house offers breathtaking views that capture the essence of coastal beauty. From spacious windows you can gaze over the Aegean as waves gently lap the shore. Stunning sunsets paint the sky in vibrant hues—a serene backdrop for relaxation. Whether over morning coffee or an evening unwind, the view promises constant tranquility and inspiration.

Amenities

Our seaside house is equipped for a comfortable stay: two covered parking spaces, Wi‑Fi throughout, a full kitchen with large fridge, stove, kettle, toaster, microwave, and French press; a second bathroom with marble-framed mirror and washing machine; cozy bedrooms, an indoor fire pit, and easy access to a nearby beach bar.
