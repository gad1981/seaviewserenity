1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar tools (such as local storage or pixels) may also be used. They help the site work, remember preferences, and understand how the site is used.

Sea View Serenity (seaviewserenity.gr) is responsible for cookies set by this website. Some cookies are set by third parties we use (for example maps, social networks, or advertising tools).

3. Cookies we use

Analytics & marketing: this site may use Facebook/Meta Pixel or similar tools to measure visits and campaign performance. These can involve advertising cookies or identifiers.

4. Legal basis

Essential cookies are used because they are necessary to provide the site. Non-essential cookies (such as marketing or non-essential analytics) are used based on your consent where required by applicable law (including GDPR for visitors in the EU/EEA).

5. How to manage cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings (block, delete, or alert you before cookies are stored). You can also use industry opt-outs for interest-based advertising where available. Blocking some cookies may affect maps or other features. For Meta/Facebook controls, see your Facebook/Instagram ad settings and Meta’s help pages.

6. How long cookies last

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a set period or until you delete them. Third-party cookies follow each provider’s retention rules.
