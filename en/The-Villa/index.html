The Villa

Meet Sea View Serenity, a coastal home a short walk from Delfini Beach. Three light-filled bedrooms with sea views, two bathrooms, a kitchen ready for home cooking, welcoming living and dining spaces, a sea-view deck with fire pit, and convenient parking. Fast WiFi and a dedicated sea-view desk make it a heaven for digital nomads — everything you need for a relaxed stay on Syros.

Bedrooms x 3

Three cozy bedrooms with sea views through spacious windows. The house sleeps up to 6 guests with five beds: one king in the main room and two single beds in each of the other two rooms. Soft bedding and a calm layout make it a welcoming retreat for families and friends.
