Bedrooms x 3

Our seaside house features three cozy bedrooms designed for comfort and relaxation, each offering stunning views of the sea through spacious windows. Accommodating up to 6 guests, the rooms include four single beds and one small double, with the option to add two additional removable single beds for larger groups. Each bedroom is furnished with plush bedding to ensure a restful night's sleep. The thoughtful layout and serene decor create a welcoming atmosphere, making it the perfect retreat for families and friends.

Bathrooms x 2

The villa features also two well-appointed bathrooms designed for convenience and comfort. The second bathroom stands out with its elegant sculptured marble-framed mirror, adding a touch of sophistication. Both bathrooms are equipped with modern fixtures and ample lighting, ensuring a pleasant experience. For added convenience, a washing machine is available, making it easy to manage laundry during your stay. These thoughtfully designed spaces provide everything you need to feel refreshed and rejuvenated throughout your visit.

Dinner Area

The dining area is designed to provide a delightful dining experience with a view. It features a spacious table that comfortably seats all guests, perfect for family meals and gatherings. Large windows surround the area, offering stunning vistas of the sea and allowing natural light to flood the space. The ambiance is enhanced by tasteful decor and comfortable seating, making every meal a memorable occasion. Whether you're enjoying a casual breakfast or a festive dinner, the dining area provides a beautiful and inviting setting.

Living Room

The living room  offers a cozy retreat with coastal elegance. It features comfortable seating and tasteful decor, providing a relaxing atmosphere after a day by the sea. Large windows fill the room with natural light and offer picturesque ocean views, enhancing the serene ambiance. Guests can relax in the inviting space, enjoying moments of tranquility and entertainment with the included TV. Whether gathering around for conversation or appreciating the calming seaside scenery, the living room is a comfortable and welcoming area in our coastal retreat.

Kitchen

The kitchen is a fully equipped haven for culinary enthusiasts and casual cooks alike. It features modern appliances including a large fridge with freezer, electric stove, microwave, toaster, and French coffee maker, ensuring convenience for preparing meals. Ample counter space and storage cabinets provide practicality, while the bright, airy atmosphere enhances the cooking experience. Guests can enjoy meals at the nearby dining area with beautiful sea views, making it a perfect setting for enjoying delicious dishes and creating memorable moments during your stay by the coast.
